CTD_SIMU_RAI — RAIR-Sim

About

RAIR-Sim is a compartmental (ODE-based) simulator of the therapeutic response to radioactive iodine (RAI) therapy in metastatic thyroid cancer. Three coupled differential equations — administered activity decay, tumor cell population dynamics, and thyroglobulin (Tg) biomarker kinetics — are solved in closed form fraction by fraction. Clinicians can explore how the number of iratherapy sessions, the interval between sessions, and the administered activity affect the simulated Tg trajectory, for both RAI-responding and RAI-refractory virtual patients. Model parameters were calibrated on a 50-patient cohort via MCMC-SAEM (Monolix). See the README for the model equations and usage.
